# ploma style

Applies to every sentence ploma writes into a manuscript, a response letter or a report. `style:` in `ploma.yaml` can add phrases and relax the em-dash rule.

- Short, direct scientific sentences. One claim per sentence. Active voice when the actor matters.
- No sentence-level em-dash or en-dash. Use a full stop, a comma, a colon or parentheses. Number ranges (`1--5`) are fine.
- No AI preamble or closing in manuscript prose: no "Certainly", "Here is", "I hope this helps", no summary of what you are about to write.
- Compact, horizontal figures: wide and short, shared axes, legends inside the plot area, no wasted whitespace.
- Prefer the precise word over the impressive one. Numbers carry units.

Forbidden phrases (plus `style.forbid_phrases`):
delve, delves, delving, leverage (as a verb), it is worth noting, it should be noted, in this paper we, crucial, pivotal, paramount, groundbreaking, cutting-edge, state-of-the-art (without a citation), novel (without a stated difference), seamless, robust (without a test), a myriad of, plethora, navigate the landscape, in the realm of, tapestry, underscore, shed light on, pave the way, game-changer, holistic, synergy, furthermore (opening a paragraph), moreover (opening a paragraph), in conclusion (outside the conclusion), to the best of our knowledge (without a search).
