## ploma

This repo uses ploma, a Claude Code plugin that runs the LaTeX paper lifecycle.

- Facts live in ploma.yaml: build, venue, style and pipeline settings. The rules under `.claude/rules/ploma-*.md` are generated from it.
- Never restate the paper's facts in CLAUDE.md. Edit ploma.yaml and re-run /ploma:onboard.
- Installed skills: /ploma:compile, /ploma:validate-bib, /ploma:audit-reproducibility, /ploma:verify-claims, /ploma:proofread, /ploma:write, /ploma:format, /ploma:review, /ploma:devils-advocate, /ploma:brainstorm, /ploma:lit-review, /ploma:grill, /ploma:spec, /ploma:respond, /ploma:doctor, /ploma:status, /ploma:help, /ploma:onboard.
- Planned (milestone 2), not installed yet, do not invoke: commit.
- Health and position: /ploma:doctor and /ploma:status.
