Reviewer #3

Questions

1. Summary of the paper. Please summarise the main contributions.
The paper proposes a score-based method to rank items from pairwise outcomes and evaluates it on 12 teams over one season.

2. Strengths. What are the main strengths of the submission?
- The method is simple and easy to reproduce.
- The results table is clear and the gain over both baselines is visible.

3. Weaknesses. What are the main weaknesses of the submission?
- The evaluation uses a single season, so the 0.81 average fitness may not generalise.
- The fitness measure is not compared with a standard ranking metric.
- Figure 1 lacks axis labels.

4. Relevance to the conference. Is the topic in scope?
Yes, ranking from pairwise data fits the conference topics.

5. Overall evaluation (-3 to 3).
1 (weak accept)

6. Reviewer confidence (1 to 5).
3 (fairly confident)

7. Comments to the authors.
Please add a second season or a cross-validation split, and report variance across runs.
