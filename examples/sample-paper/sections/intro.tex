\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Ranking items from noisy pairwise outcomes is a common task~\cite{Bradley1952}.
Rating systems update a score after each outcome~\cite{Elo1978}.
We propose a method that fits a score per item and reports how well the ranking explains the data.
On 12 teams it reaches an average fitness of 0.81 (Section~\ref{sec:results}).
